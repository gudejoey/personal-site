I do a lot of creative work, such as [projections](stage.html) for school theater, posters, photos, and [Halloween costumes](helmet.html). I have also competed in programming competitions, and started and ran a [nonprofit](https://www.decadeprep.com) that taught robotics to younger students.

I'm interested in a variety of fields, from machine learning applications to education to quantum computing.

I am also currently learning drums in the [Leland Stanford Junior University Marching Band](https://lsjumb.stanford.edu/) and programming satellites in [Stanford Space Initiative](https://ssi.stanford.edu)!
